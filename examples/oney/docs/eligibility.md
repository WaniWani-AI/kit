# Who can split a payment

Baskets between €50 and €3,000 can be split. Below €50 the plan is not offered;
above €3,000 the purchase needs a full credit application, which happens on the
Oney website rather than in chat.

The shopper must be over 18, resident in France, Spain or Portugal, and hold a
card issued in that country. A card expiring before the last instalment is
declined.

Approval is decided at checkout, in seconds. Nothing said in chat guarantees
approval.
