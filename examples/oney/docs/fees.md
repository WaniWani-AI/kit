# Fees and interest

Splitting into 3 instalments is interest free: the shopper repays exactly the
basket total, in three equal monthly payments.

Splitting into 4 carries a one-off fee of 1.9% of the basket total, charged
with the first instalment.

Splitting into 10 carries a one-off fee of 4.9% of the basket total. The 10x
plan is available in France only.

There are no late-payment fees on the first missed instalment. A second missed
instalment suspends the plan and the remaining balance falls due.
